Project

2024.09–2025.12 · Industry Collaboration with OKESTRO

VIP — Voice Interview Partner

Gamified voice-based AI mock interview application

GitHub

Overview

VIP combines AI interview practice with a game-like progression model. Instead of simple question-and-answer practice, the application uses levels, missions, scores, feedback, and replay to encourage repeated interview training.

The level structure ranges from basic questions to one-to-one interviews, multi-interviewer sessions, and pressure interviews.

My Role

  • Organized interview datasets by job type and difficulty.
  • Developed Flutter UI/UX for the mobile application.
  • Implemented game-oriented interview scenarios and interaction flows.

Key Features

  • Voice-based interview practice
  • STT and TTS interaction
  • Level-based interview progression
  • Score and feedback system
  • Interview history and user progress management
