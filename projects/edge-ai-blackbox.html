Project

2025.02–2025.05 · Industry Collaboration with Qualcomm

Edge-AI Blackbox Assistance

On-device AI-based smart vehicle safety blackbox system

GitHub

Overview

The project analyzes driver voice and the surrounding environment to detect lane departure, vehicle warnings, abnormal behavior, horns, sirens, and other safety events. The system was designed around Rubik Pi 3 for real-time on-device inference, with local processing intended to improve response time, network independence, and privacy.

My Role

  • Developed emergency audio-event models for screams, horns, and sirens.
  • Integrated ADXL345 acceleration sensing for collision and rollover detection.
  • Worked on lightweight audio-model optimization with Edge Impulse.
  • Contributed to Android app UI design for MyPage, HOME, and Events.

Key Features

  • Lane-departure and vehicle-event detection
  • Collision / rollover sensing
  • Emergency sound detection
  • Galaxy Watch alerts using voice and vibration
  • Android event history and reporting
