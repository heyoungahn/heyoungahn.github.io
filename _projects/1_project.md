**2025 · Industry Collaboration: Qualcomm**

A smart vehicle safety system that analyzes driver voice and the surrounding environment in real time.

Key functions include lane-departure detection, vehicle warning, abnormal-behavior detection, horn and siren recognition, automatic video saving, and mobile-app linkage. The system was designed for on-device inference on **Rubik Pi**, with an emphasis on low-latency response and privacy-preserving deployment.

**Tech:** Rubik Pi · On-device AI · Computer Vision · Audio Recognition · Edge AI
