Semiconductor Vision Inspection — MONETA

MONETA is a semiconductor vision inspection study focused on single-reference BGA automated optical inspection (AOI).

The research investigates how reliable defect inspection can be performed with limited reference data while maintaining practical deployment on CPU-based industrial vision systems.

Research Focus

  • Single-reference inspection
  • BGA defect inspection
  • Semiconductor AOI
  • CPU-based machine vision
  • Statistical defect detection
  • Inspection under limited reference data

Physical AI for Smart Manufacturing

I am researching Physical AI systems for autonomous inspection and response in smart manufacturing environments. The current study focuses on a humanoid robot that patrols semiconductor manufacturing equipment, cooperates with a central monitoring system, and acquires HMI information from target equipment.

Current Research Scenario

  1. Humanoid PatrolThe humanoid continuously patrols multiple SAW machines.
  2. Equipment Status ObservationThe robot observes equipment states such as the Stack Light.
  3. Abnormal Event / Operator RequestAn abnormal event occurs, or an operator requests inspection of a specific SAW machine.
  4. Central Monitoring DecisionThe monitoring system integrates equipment events and operator requests to determine the inspection target.
  5. Target Equipment ApproachThe humanoid moves to the selected SAW equipment.
  6. HMI InspectionThe robot captures the HMI screen and transmits the information to the central monitoring system.
  7. Task ContinuationAfter confirmation, the robot performs the next task or resumes its patrol.

Current Validation

  • Humanoid robot simulation
  • Semiconductor manufacturing environment
  • SAW equipment inspection scenario
  • Isaac Sim-based validation
  • Robot patrol and stop behavior
  • Central monitoring workflow
  • HMI inspection scenario
