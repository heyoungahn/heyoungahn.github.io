Semiconductor Vision Inspection — MONETA

MONETA is a semiconductor vision inspection study focused on single-reference BGA automated optical inspection (AOI).

The research investigates how reliable defect inspection can be performed with limited reference data while maintaining practical deployment on CPU-based industrial vision systems.

Research Focus

  • Single-reference inspection
  • BGA defect inspection
  • Semiconductor AOI
  • CPU-based machine vision
  • Statistical defect detection
  • Inspection under limited reference data

Physical AI for Smart Manufacturing

I am researching Physical AI systems for autonomous inspection and response in smart manufacturing environments. The current study focuses on a humanoid robot that patrols semiconductor manufacturing equipment, cooperates with a central monitoring system, and acquires HMI information from target equipment.
