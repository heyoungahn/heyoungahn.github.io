**2024**

A camera-based medication assistance application using **YOLOv8** for pill recognition.

The application provides medication information such as dosage, side effects, and precautions, along with pharmacy search, medication reminders, and chatbot assistance.

**Award:** Bronze Award, 20th Hansung University Engineering Competition, 2024

**Tech:** YOLOv8 · Computer Vision · Object Detection
