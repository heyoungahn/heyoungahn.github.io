I am an M.S. student at **AML Lab, Hansung University**, working on AI for smart manufacturing.

My research focuses on **computer vision for semiconductor inspection** and **physical AI for robotic manufacturing automation**, with particular interests in reliable visual inspection, long-horizon manipulation, simulation-based validation, and resource-constrained AI.

### Research Interests

**Computer Vision** · **Smart Manufacturing** · **Physical AI** · **Edge AI**
