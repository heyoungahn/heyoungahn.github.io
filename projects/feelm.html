Feelm

Personalized movie recommendation and diary application

GitHubDemo Video

Overview

Feelm combines a personal movie diary with recommendation functionality. Rather than relying only on search or ratings, the project uses user diary records, preferences, genres, runtime, and ratings to provide a more personalized movie experience.

My Role

  • Designed a recommendation approach combining content-based filtering and clustering.
  • Developed Flutter front-end UI/UX.
  • Implemented movie-diary and personalized recommendation functions.

Key Features

  • Firebase account management
  • Calendar-based movie diary
  • Movie search and detailed information
  • Personalized recommendations
  • Reviews and favorites
